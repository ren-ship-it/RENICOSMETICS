# Reni Cosmetics — Design Brainstorm

## Approach 1: Obsidian Apothecary
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Luxury pharmaceutical meets haute couture — think Hermès meets a clinical research lab.

**Core Principles:**
- Deep obsidian black (#1C1C1E) as the dominant canvas
- Champagne gold (#C9A96E) used sparingly as a precision accent
- Typography as the primary visual element — no decorative imagery
- "Pharmaceutical restraint" — every element earns its place

**Color Philosophy:** Black is not dark; it is authoritative. Gold is not decorative; it is a signal of precision and rarity. Alabaster white (#F5F5F0) appears only as a contrast surface, never as a background.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial grid. Full-bleed sections with hard horizontal cuts. No rounded corners anywhere. Generous negative space.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin gold horizontal rules as section dividers
- Large Playfair Display numerals (01, 02...) as section markers
- Monospaced ingredient percentages in a fixed-width font

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states reveal information, not decoration. Scroll-triggered reveals are slow and deliberate. Nothing bounces.

**Animation:** Fade-in on scroll (opacity 0→1, translateY 20px→0). Duration 600ms. Ease-out. No spring physics.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — headlines, product names, quotes
- Body: DM Sans — descriptions, specs, UI labels
- Mono: Space Mono — ingredient percentages, technical data
</text>

## Approach 2: Clinical White Room
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Scandinavian minimalism filtered through a Parisian dermatology clinic.

**Core Principles:**
- Pure white (#FFFFFF) canvas with deep navy (#0A1628) type
- Cerulean blue (#1A3A5C) as the sole accent
- Extreme whitespace — content breathes
- Grid-based precision — every element aligned to an 8px grid

**Color Philosophy:** White communicates sterility, trust, and premium positioning. Navy provides gravitas. Cerulean signals "science" without feeling cold.

**Layout Paradigm:** Centered editorial column (max-width 960px) with full-bleed photography breaking out of the column at key moments.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin navy border lines
- Oversized Playfair Display pull-quotes
- Photography: extreme close-up skin texture

**Interaction Philosophy:** Understated. Hover states are a 1px border change. Transitions are 200ms.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display — headlines

## Approach 3: Dark Science Editorial ← SELECTED
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** High-fashion editorial meets molecular biology textbook — the aesthetic of a luxury brand that happens to be built in a lab.

**Core Principles:**
- Obsidian (#1C1C1E) and Alabaster (#F5F5F0) as the two primary surfaces — alternating sections
- Champagne gold (#C9A96E) as the singular accent — used only for emphasis, never decoration
- Deep Cerulean (#1A3A5C) for interactive elements and scientific callouts
- Typography-first design — the words ARE the design

**Color Philosophy:** The alternating dark/light sections mirror the AM/PM protocol structure of the brand itself. Dark = night ritual, clinical depth. Light = morning clarity, scientific precision.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width sections with hard horizontal breaks. Left-aligned type. Asymmetric content placement. Wide margins that create "breathing room" around key claims.

**Signature Elements:**
- Gold horizontal rules (1–2px) as section dividers and emphasis lines
- Large Playfair Display numerals as structural anchors
- Monospaced percentage figures in Space Mono for ingredient data

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover reveals mechanism descriptions. Scroll-triggered section reveals. The site should feel like turning pages in a scientific journal — deliberate, not playful.

**Animation:** Staggered fade-in on scroll. translateY(30px) → translateY(0), opacity 0→1. 700ms ease-out. Stagger delay 100ms between sibling elements.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (serif) — product names, headlines, pull-quotes
- Body: DM Sans — all body copy, UI labels, navigation
- Mono: Space Mono — ingredient percentages, pH values, technical specs
</text>

**SELECTED: Approach 3 — Dark Science Editorial**

The alternating Obsidian/Alabaster section structure mirrors the brand's AM/PM protocol architecture, creating a thematic coherence between the design and the product philosophy. Typography-first design with Playfair Display + DM Sans + Space Mono creates the "luxury pharmaceutical" aesthetic that differentiates Reni Cosmetics from conventional beauty brands.
